## Preview use

Current releases are testing previews for Windows x64 and Apple silicon Macs. Features and file handling can change. Review the [installation guide](installation.md), [Google Drive guide](drive.md), and release notes, and back up important files before editing them or running programs against them. Review AI changes before relying on them.

## Connected services and accounts

Drive connections, Git remotes, and AI CLIs use the accounts and services you configure. Those services have their own terms, permissions, availability, charges, and data-retention rules. Installing irori does not supply a model subscription or authorize use of other people's files.

Review [Privacy and data handling](privacy.md) before connecting Drive or running AI, terminal commands, or routines. The in-app data-use confirmations explain possible transfers and retained copies; they do not verify provider compliance or replace tool permissions.

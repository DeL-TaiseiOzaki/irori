# irori 0.1.43 — Moving between the Overview and a brain

The last step of the new design (v5) is about movement and finish.

- **Zoom between levels.** Opening a brain from the Overview's map zooms in: the
  map grows away around the brain you chose while the brain's page settles in.
  Going back to the Overview zooms out.
- **Sheets and requests.** Sheets such as Brain settings and the Drive
  connection rise into place over a fading backdrop. A permission request from
  an AI arrives with a small spring before it starts to breathe.
- **Less motion.** When your system asks for reduced motion, the levels switch
  at once and every loop — rings, glows, sparks, the floating map — stays still.
- **Finish.**
  - Schema files keep their names in the brain's tree (`AGENTS.md`), while notes
    still read as titles.
  - The brain's home lists the instruction files first, and its crumb shows a
    house.
  - A small graph appears at its own size instead of filling the page.
  - The Overview's buttons and your AI's header match the design.

**Fixed:** opening the backlinks list while the count on the link button was
being refreshed could show 「新しい検索に切り替わりました」 instead of the list, because
the two looked at the KB separately and the second cancelled the first. They now
share one look.

## Installing

**Windows 11 x64:** download `irori-0.1.43-windows-x64-Setup.exe` and run it. The
installer carries no distribution signature, so Windows shows a warning that the
publisher cannot be verified.

**Mac (Apple silicon):** download `irori-0.1.43-macos-arm64.dmg`, open it, and
drag **irori** into **アプリケーション**. Intel Macs are not supported. The first
launch is refused because the package carries no distribution signature. Open
**システム設定 → プライバシーとセキュリティ**, find irori's blocked notice under
セキュリティ, and press **このまま開く**. Later launches behave normally. Keep
irori in **アプリケーション**: the in-app update replaces the app where it is.

An installed 0.1.29 or later offers this version with **更新して再起動**.

## Verification

The Overview test now checks the zoom into a brain and that nothing zooms when
reduced motion is asked for. A new script, `scripts/ui-screens.ts`, captures the
main screens in each theme and language so they can be compared with the
design canvas; this release was compared in the hearth, dark and light themes.

## Remaining boundaries

Passing CI is not acceptance on an installed device. The zoom starts from the
brain you click on the map; from the rail or the columns it starts from the
middle.

Read [the distribution notes](https://github.com/DeL-TaiseiOzaki/irori/blob/main/docs/DISTRIBUTION.md)
and [the full release plan](https://github.com/DeL-TaiseiOzaki/irori/blob/main/docs/RELEASE-PLAN.md).
Report the displayed version, OS and exact error through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/DeL-TaiseiOzaki/irori/issues), omitting private
notes, credentials and OAuth URLs. Source is MIT licensed; dependencies retain
their upstream terms.

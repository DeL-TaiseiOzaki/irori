# irori 0.1.87 preview 1 — Categories of your own

A hibachi's category is no longer limited to personal, team and organization.
In **hibachi の設定** and when registering a hibachi, type any name, such as
`研究室` or `Client A`, or pick one of the presets or a category another
hibachi of the workspace already has. The name is kept in `.irori/scope.json`,
so everyone who opens the knowledge base sees it. A preset typed in English or
Japanese is the same category. On the Overview map, each category of your own
is its own row after the presets.

`irori add`, `irori create` and `irori clone` take any name with `--category`.

A knowledge base with a category of its own cannot be opened by irori 0.1.86
or earlier; update every device first.

## Verification

`npm run build`, `npm test` and the Electron UI suites pass. Unit tests cover
storing and reading a category of the KB's own, refusing a blank, multi-line or
over-long one, preset names in both languages, and the map's row order and
non-overlapping areas; the brain settings smoke types a new category and a
preset in English through the sheet. Not tried with Windows, macOS or a real
knowledge base.

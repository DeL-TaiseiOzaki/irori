# irori 0.2.84 preview 1 — A tab's right click, and one workspace in two tabs

- Right-click a workspace tab for what a browser's tab offers
  ([ADR 066](../decisions/066-tab-menu-and-duplicate-tabs.md)): **タブを複製**,
  **新しいウィンドウに移動**, **タブを閉じる**, **他のタブを閉じる** and
  **右側のタブを閉じる**.
- **タブを複製** opens the same workspace again as a new tab, where the tab was. The
  two tabs keep their own vault, view and level; the workspace's agents and
  conversations are shared, and closing one of its tabs stops none of them.
- Closing several tabs at once asks once, only when something would stop.
- A tab dragged onto another window that has its workspace open is added there as
  a tab of its own, instead of showing the one already there.

## Verification

`npm run build`, `npm test` and `npm run test:ui` pass on Linux under Xvfb. A new
smoke, `scripts/tab-menu-ui-smoke.ts`, duplicates a tab and checks each copy keeps
its own vault, closes one tab, the others and those to the right from the menu,
moves a tab to a new window from it, and drops that tab back beside a tab of its
workspace. `tests/workspace-tabs.test.ts` covers closing several tabs and what a
workspace open in two tabs keeps. Not tried on macOS or Windows.

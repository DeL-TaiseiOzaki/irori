# irori 0.1.38 — The new look: one brain at a time

irori starts moving to its new design (v5). This first step changes how the
workspace is laid out and keeps everything you could do before.

- **Brains on the left.** Each KB you open is a _brain_, shown as a tile on the
  narrow rail at the left, in your workspace's order. Choose a tile to show
  that brain. Hover a tile to see its name and what its AI is doing; a turning
  ring means its AI is working, an orange dot means it waits for your answer.
- **One panel per brain.** The panel beside the rail holds the brain's
  **Schema**, **Knowledge** and **Contents** together. Each section folds to
  its heading and can be resized. **変更** (Changes) replaces the files with the
  brain's Git changes; a diff opens on the page with line numbers.
- **The page.** The bar above a note shows where it is (brain › layer ›
  folders › note), whether it is saved, its backlinks, its details (location
  and the lines a person wrote) and a menu with rename and move, delete, reload,
  table or source view, code assistance, and materials and outputs.
- **The AI panel** names the brain's Schema it works from, shows each step it
  takes, and asks for permission in an orange card. **＋ 参照** adds the open
  note as a reference.
- **Settings** moved to the rail's bottom button: theme, Markdown font,
  language, updates, and restoring pending uploads. A new theme, **いろり**
  (Hearth), keeps the chrome dark and the page like paper; it is what
  "match system" shows in a light desktop.
- **Shortcuts.** Ctrl+K (⌘K) opens search, and Ctrl+` opens or closes the
  terminal.
- The Overview on the rail is reserved for the next steps, when several
  brains' AIs can work at once.

## Installing

**Windows 11 x64:** download `irori-0.1.38-windows-x64-Setup.exe` and run it. The
installer carries no distribution signature, so Windows shows a warning that the
publisher cannot be verified.

**Mac (Apple silicon):** download `irori-0.1.38-macos-arm64.dmg`, open it, and
drag **irori** into **アプリケーション**. Intel Macs are not supported. The first
launch is refused because the package carries no distribution signature. Open
**システム設定 → プライバシーとセキュリティ**, find irori's blocked notice under
セキュリティ, and press **このまま開く**. Later launches behave normally. Keep
irori in **アプリケーション**: the in-app update replaces the app where it is.

An installed 0.1.29 or later offers this version with **更新して再起動**.

## Verification

The Electron UI tests were rewritten for the rail and the brain panel and still
check what they checked before: switching brains saves the note being edited,
each brain's Schema stays its own, sections resize and are remembered, source
control stages, commits, pushes, merges and resolves conflicts, the note's
actions work from its menu, and the English interface shows no Japanese.

## Remaining boundaries

Passing CI is not acceptance on an installed device. The start screen, the
graph, materials, search and Drive dialogs keep their earlier look until the
next steps of the redesign.

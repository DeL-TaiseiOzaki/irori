# irori 0.1.32 — Resize the Schema, knowledge and materials panes yourself

The sidebar's panes had fixed proportions. Now the lines between them can be
dragged: the borders between **Schema**, the knowledge row, the materials row
and the workspace's **Google Drive** list change their heights, and the line
between the personal and team panes changes their widths. Each size is
remembered on this device. Folding both panes of a row hands its height to the
other rows, and dragging a row down to its heading folds it. The Drive list,
which had a fixed height, is now one of these rows, and the note buttons below
it (今日のノート, 削除したノートを復元) sit under the whole explorer.

On a tall window the sidebar also stopped short of the bottom edge when its
content was short; it now fills the window.

The width of the sidebar and the AI panel, when the AI panel was open, was not
remembered after a restart, because the saved key was longer than the settings
file allowed. It is kept now.

## Installing

**Windows 11 x64:** download `irori-0.1.32-windows-x64-Setup.exe` and run it. The
installer carries no distribution signature, so Windows shows a warning that the
publisher cannot be verified.

**Mac (Apple silicon):** download `irori-0.1.32-macos-arm64.dmg`, open it, and
drag **irori** into **アプリケーション**. Intel Macs are not supported. The first
launch is refused because the package carries no distribution signature. Open
**システム設定 → プライバシーとセキュリティ**, find irori's blocked notice under
セキュリティ, and press **このまま開く**. The button appears only just after an
attempt to open the app, so if it is missing, open irori again and return to
that pane. Later launches behave normally. Keep irori in **アプリケーション**:
the in-app update replaces the app where it is.

## Verification

The published files are the artifacts of the CI run for the commit named under
**Exact package** below, unchanged except for their download names. The layered
explorer UI test drags the row borders, the Drive border and the personal/team
border, checks that the sizes change and are written to the device settings,
and folds and unfolds a whole row. A settings test stores the 67-character key the pane
library writes for the three-pane workspace.

## Remaining boundaries

Passing CI is not acceptance on an installed device. Pane sizes are kept per
device, not per workspace.

Read [the distribution notes](https://github.com/DeL-TaiseiOzaki/irori/blob/main/docs/DISTRIBUTION.md)

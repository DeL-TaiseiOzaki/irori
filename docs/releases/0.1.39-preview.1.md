# irori 0.1.39 — A brain's home, graph, materials and search in the new design

The second step of the new design (v5). What used to open in separate windows
now takes the page, beside the brain's panel and its AI.

- **Home.** Choosing a brain, or its name in the panel or above the page, shows
  its home: the brain's name, category, folder and branch; today's note, a new
  note and the terminal; its Schema (instruction files and skills, with the
  skills' reach check), its Knowledge (the top folders and notes, and the
  graph), its Contents (the Drive folders with their access and uploads); the
  latest changes, with a way to commit; and the latest AI runs, with a way to
  the materials and outputs. It replaces the old welcome.
- **Graph.** The ontology opens on the page instead of in a window: filters
  above, the graph on a dotted canvas with the chosen entity's details in a
  card, and the entity list and CSV files behind the table button. The index's
  freshness and its update sit in the bar above.
- **Materials and outputs** open on the page too, with the runs on the left and
  registering an output and preparing a Drive upload on the right.
- **Backlinks.** The link button above a note shows how many notes lead to it;
  pressing it lists them.
- **Search (Ctrl+K / ⌘K)** opens a palette: type, press Enter, choose a result
  with the arrow keys and press Enter again to open it. The brain to search is a
  chip; the matched words are marked.
- **Google Drive** connections open as one sheet with the three steps (account,
  folder, place in Contents) beside the folders already connected.
- **The start screen** shows your workspaces with their brains, lets you combine
  brains into a new workspace, and adds a brain from a folder or from GitHub.

## Installing

**Windows 11 x64:** download `irori-0.1.39-windows-x64-Setup.exe` and run it. The
installer carries no distribution signature, so Windows shows a warning that the
publisher cannot be verified.

**Mac (Apple silicon):** download `irori-0.1.39-macos-arm64.dmg`, open it, and
drag **irori** into **アプリケーション**. Intel Macs are not supported. The first
launch is refused because the package carries no distribution signature. Open
**システム設定 → プライバシーとセキュリティ**, find irori's blocked notice under
セキュリティ, and press **このまま開く**. Later launches behave normally. Keep
irori in **アプリケーション**: the in-app update replaces the app where it is.

An installed 0.1.29 or later offers this version with **更新して再起動**.

## Verification

The Electron UI tests follow the views onto the page and still prove what they
proved: the graph's filters, note links, external changes and index updates;
the materials' records, kept versions, relinking and Drive preparation;
backlinks that stay current; search isolation, stale answers and conflicts; the
Drive connection steps; and the start screen's workspaces and registration.

## Remaining boundaries

Passing CI is not acceptance on an installed device. Search looks through one
brain at a time; searching every brain at once comes with the Overview.

Read [the distribution notes](https://github.com/DeL-TaiseiOzaki/irori/blob/main/docs/DISTRIBUTION.md)
and [the full release plan](https://github.com/DeL-TaiseiOzaki/irori/blob/main/docs/RELEASE-PLAN.md).
Report the displayed version, OS and exact error through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/DeL-TaiseiOzaki/irori/issues), omitting private
notes, credentials and OAuth URLs. Source is MIT licensed; dependencies retain

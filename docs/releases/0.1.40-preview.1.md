# irori 0.1.40 — Give each brain its own name, icon and colour

Each brain (each KB you open) now has a look you choose, and everyone who opens
the same KB sees it.

- **Brain の設定.** Open it from the brain's menu (⋯ beside its name) or the
  pencil beside the name on its home. Change the **name**, the **category**
  (個人, チーム, 組織 or none), the **icon** — one of 24 symbols, one or two
  characters or an emoji, or an image (PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 2 MB) — and
  one of eight **colours**: 石, 藍, 苔, 青磁, 藤, 桜, 砂 and 白. Previews show how
  the brain will look on the rail, on the map and above a note.
- **Kept in the KB.** The choice is written to the KB's `.irori/scope.json`
  (an image goes beside it as `.irori/icon-….png`), so it travels with the KB
  through Git. Fields irori does not know are kept as they are.
- Until you choose, a brain keeps showing the first character of its name on a
  colour of its own, as before.
- The settings cannot change while the brain's AI runs, a Git operation or a
  Drive connection is in progress.

**Older versions and "none".** A KB whose category is set to none cannot be
opened by irori 0.1.39 or earlier, which require a category. Update every
device that opens the KB, or keep a category.

## Installing

**Windows 11 x64:** download `irori-0.1.40-windows-x64-Setup.exe` and run it. The
installer carries no distribution signature, so Windows shows a warning that the
publisher cannot be verified.

**Mac (Apple silicon):** download `irori-0.1.40-macos-arm64.dmg`, open it, and
drag **irori** into **アプリケーション**. Intel Macs are not supported. The first
launch is refused because the package carries no distribution signature. Open
**システム設定 → プライバシーとセキュリティ**, find irori's blocked notice under
セキュリティ, and press **このまま開く**. Later launches behave normally. Keep
irori in **アプリケーション**: the in-app update replaces the app where it is.

An installed 0.1.29 or later offers this version with **更新して再起動**.

## Verification

Unit tests write a name, a category and a look and read them back after a
restart, keep unknown fields, remove a category or a look, open a declaration
from before looks existed, refuse a colour or an icon outside the offered set
without touching the file, keep only the named image icon, and refuse a
`scope.json` that links out of the KB. A new UI test changes each part in the
sheet, checks the two-character limit, cancels, sets an image, and restarts.

## Remaining boundaries

Passing CI is not acceptance on an installed device. A change made on another
device arrives through Git; the open window shows it after the KB is opened
again.

Read [the distribution notes](https://github.com/DeL-TaiseiOzaki/irori/blob/main/docs/DISTRIBUTION.md)
and [the full release plan](https://github.com/DeL-TaiseiOzaki/irori/blob/main/docs/RELEASE-PLAN.md).
Report the displayed version, OS and exact error through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/DeL-TaiseiOzaki/irori/issues), omitting private
notes, credentials and OAuth URLs. Source is MIT licensed; dependencies retain
their upstream terms.

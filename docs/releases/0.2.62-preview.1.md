# irori 0.2.62 preview 1 — sheet tabs along the bottom

- A spreadsheet (`.xlsx`, `.xlsm`, `.xls`, `.ods`) now fills the stage as in
  Excel. Its sheet tabs sit in one row along the lower edge of the grid and stay
  in view however far the grid is scrolled or zoomed; the grid scrolls inside the
  viewer instead of the page. More sheets than fit scroll sideways.
- The viewer's zoom now scales the cells, not the grid's frame, so zooming in no
  longer pushes the grid past the bottom of the window.
- Choosing another sheet starts it at its first cell rather than at the last
  sheet's scroll position.

## Verification

`npm run build`, `npm run format:check`, `npm test` and `npm run test:ui` pass.
`viewers-ui-smoke` now scrolls the grid to its end, zooms in and switches sheets,
and checks each time that the tabs lie below the grid, inside the stage, with
nothing for the stage itself to scroll. On Linux under Xvfb, the tabs were also
measured at 50 % and 200 % zoom and in a short window, before and after the
change.

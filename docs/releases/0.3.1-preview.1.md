# irori 0.3.1 preview 1 — Close a session from the session board

- Each card on the session board now has a ×, where its state dot is while you
  point at or focus the card, as a VS Code tab shows its close button. It closes
  that conversation: the card leaves the board and the conversation leaves
  every column that showed it. A column left with nothing opens an empty
  conversation.
- On a conversation that is running or waiting for you, the × reads
  停止して閉じる (Stop and close) and stops its run first. Its end does not put
  the card back.
- The conversation stays in the history. It shows on the board again when you
  open it again or send it another instruction. Instructions still waiting to be
  sent are kept for then.

## Verification

`tests/session-board.test.ts` checks that a closed conversation shows in no
lane whatever its state, and that a hand-off still at work stands on its own
once the conversation that made it is closed. `scripts/session-board-ui-smoke.ts`
closes an idle card, then a card whose Pi fixture run is still going (the card
goes and stays gone after the run ends), then every card left, and checks the
column is left with an empty conversation. Not tried with a real CLI agent.

# irori 0.2.53 preview 1 — one smooth title bar, and settings in a window of their own

- The window's top is irori's own. The system title bar and the menu bar
  above the app are gone; the workspace tabs sit in a single quiet bar that
  also moves the window, and double-clicking it maximises.
- Minimise, maximise and close keep their place on Windows and Linux, now in
  irori's colours, and follow the theme as you change it. On Windows 11 the
  accent-coloured outline is gone. On macOS the traffic lights sit in the same
  bar.
- On Windows and Linux the menu (New Window, Edit, View, Window) opens from the
  menu button at the bar's left; its shortcuts work as before. The menu's names
  are now in the language you chose.
- A dark window no longer flashes light while it opens.
- Settings open as their own sheet in the middle of the window, over the
  blurred app, instead of a small popover by the rail. Their parts — General,
  Notes, Agents, Routines, Account and Updates — are listed at the side; choose
  one to jump to it. Escape, the close button or a click outside closes them.

## Verification

`npm run build`, `npm test` (new `window-frame`) and `npm run test:ui` pass.
On Linux under Xvfb the real window was captured with the system's controls in
the hearth and light themes, switching theme while open, and the menu opened
from the menu button. The settings sheet was captured in hearth, light and dark,
in both languages, at the smallest window size and from the start screen, with
the side list jumping and a backdrop click closing it. Not yet seen on a
Windows or macOS desktop.

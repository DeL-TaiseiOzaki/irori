# irori 0.1.41 — See every brain at once, and let their AIs work side by side

The brains of a workspace now have an **Overview**, and each brain's AI keeps
working while you look at another brain.

- **Several AIs at once.** While one brain's AI runs, or has instructions
  waiting, you can open another brain and start its AI. Each brain remembers
  which AI (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Pi) you chose for it. Instructions
  waiting behind a run are sent when that run finishes, even if you are looking
  at another brain; a stopped or failed run still pauses them for you to resume.
- **Requests stay answerable.** A permission or question an AI is waiting on can
  be answered after you switch away and back, or from the Overview.
- **全体 (Overview)** — the map button at the top of the left rail:
  - **地図 (Map):** every brain with what its AI is doing, grouped softly by
    個人 / チーム / 組織 when brains have a category. A dashed line joins two
    brains when an AI in one used notes from the other, named by the note.
    Click a brain to open it.
  - **Brain の AI:** beside the map, each brain's AI with its state, the request
    it waits on, **停止** and **送信を再開**. Choose a brain and type to send it
    an instruction, or add it to that brain's queue while it runs.
  - **並列 (Columns):** the brains side by side with their AI, Schema, Knowledge
    and Contents. A note opens in its brain.
- **Search every brain.** ⌘K / Ctrl+K has a **すべての Brain** choice (the default
  when opened from the Overview) that shows results grouped by brain. Searches
  in different brains no longer cancel each other.
- The status bar's AI summary leads to the Overview while another brain's AI is
  working. The workspace can be left once no brain's AI is running.

## Installing

**Windows 11 x64:** download `irori-0.1.41-windows-x64-Setup.exe` and run it. The
installer carries no distribution signature, so Windows shows a warning that the
publisher cannot be verified.

**Mac (Apple silicon):** download `irori-0.1.41-macos-arm64.dmg`, open it, and
drag **irori** into **アプリケーション**. Intel Macs are not supported. The first
launch is refused because the package carries no distribution signature. Open
**システム設定 → プライバシーとセキュリティ**, find irori's blocked notice under
セキュリティ, and press **このまま開く**. Later launches behave normally. Keep
irori in **アプリケーション**: the in-app update replaces the app where it is.

An installed 0.1.29 or later offers this version with **更新して再起動**.

## Verification

A new UI test runs two protocol-fixture CLIs in two brains at once: it switches
brains while one waits for permission and one runs, answers the waiting request
after switching and from the Overview, checks that the waiting brain's queue
goes on by itself while another brain is shown, queues from the Overview behind
a run, stops that run and resumes its queue, sends from the Overview to an idle
brain, and opens a brain from the map and a note from the columns. Unit tests
cover the map layout, the reference lines, two brains running with live
requests, and scans of different KBs running side by side. No real model runs
in these tests.

## Remaining boundaries

Passing CI is not acceptance on an installed device, and real CLIs have not
been run in two brains at once for this release. The Overview sends with each
brain's default access mode. The map's layout is a simple first one; your AI
(the person's own agent at the centre of the map) comes in a later release.

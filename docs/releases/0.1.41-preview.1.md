# irori 0.1.41 — The new design: brains, their AIs side by side, and an Overview

irori moves to its new design (v5). Every KB you open is now a **brain**, shown
on a rail at the left; each brain's AI can work while you look at another one;
and an **Overview** shows every brain of the workspace at once. Everything you
could do in 0.1.37 is still there, in a new place.

This preview carries four steps of work. 0.1.38, 0.1.39 and 0.1.40 were
prepared but not published on their own; their notes are kept in the
repository ([0.1.38](https://github.com/DeL-TaiseiOzaki/irori/blob/main/docs/releases/0.1.38-preview.1.md),
[0.1.39](https://github.com/DeL-TaiseiOzaki/irori/blob/main/docs/releases/0.1.39-preview.1.md),
[0.1.40](https://github.com/DeL-TaiseiOzaki/irori/blob/main/docs/releases/0.1.40-preview.1.md)).

## The layout

- **Brains on the left.** The rail shows your workspace's brains as tiles, in
  its order. Choose a tile to show that brain. Hover a tile to see its name and
  what its AI is doing: a turning ring means its AI is working, an orange dot
  means it waits for your answer.
- **One panel per brain** holds its **Schema**, **Knowledge** and **Contents**
  together; each section folds and can be resized. **変更** (Changes) replaces
  the files with the brain's Git changes, and a diff opens on the page.
- **The page.** The bar above a note shows where it is (brain › layer › folders
  › note), whether it is saved, its backlinks, its details (location and the
  lines a person wrote) and a menu with rename and move, delete, reload, table
  or source view, code assistance, and materials and outputs.
- **The AI panel** names the brain's Schema it works from, shows each step it
  takes and asks for permission in an orange card. **＋ 参照** adds the open note
  as a reference.
- **Settings** moved to the rail's bottom button: theme, Markdown font,
  language, updates, and restoring pending uploads. A new theme, **いろり**
  (Hearth), keeps the chrome dark and the page like paper.
- **Shortcuts.** Ctrl+K (⌘K) opens search; Ctrl+` opens or closes the terminal.

## A brain's views

- **Home.** Choosing a brain, or its name, shows its home: name, category,
  folder and branch; today's note, a new note and the terminal; its Schema,
  Knowledge and Contents at a glance; the latest changes and AI runs. It
  replaces the old welcome.
- **Graph** and **materials and outputs** open on the page instead of in
  separate windows. The link button above a note counts its backlinks and lists
  them.
- **Search** is a palette: type, press Enter, pick a result with the arrow keys
  and press Enter again. Matched words are marked.
- **Google Drive** connections open as one sheet (account, folder, place in
  Contents) beside the folders already connected.
- **The start screen** shows your workspaces with their brains, combines brains
  into a new workspace, and adds a brain from a folder or from GitHub.

## Each brain's look

- **Brain の設定** (the brain's ⋯ menu, or the pencil on its home) changes its
  **name**, its **category** (個人, チーム, 組織 or none), its **icon** — one of
  24 symbols, one or two characters or an emoji, or an image (PNG, JPEG, GIF or
  WebP, up to 2 MB) — and one of eight **colours**: 石, 藍, 苔, 青磁, 藤, 桜, 砂
  and 白.
- The choice is written to the KB's `.irori/scope.json` (an image beside it in
  `.irori/`), so everyone who opens the KB sees the same look. Until you choose,
  a brain shows the first character of its name on a colour of its own.
- **Older versions and "none".** A KB whose category is set to none cannot be
  opened by irori 0.1.37 or earlier, which require a category. Update every
  device that opens the KB, or keep a category.

## Several AIs at once, and the Overview

- **Several AIs at once.** While one brain's AI runs, or has instructions
  waiting, you can open another brain and start its AI. Each brain remembers
  which AI (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Pi) you chose for it. Instructions
  waiting behind a run are sent when that run finishes, even if you are looking
  at another brain; a stopped or failed run still pauses them for you to resume.
- **Requests stay answerable.** A permission or question an AI is waiting on can
  be answered after you switch away and back, or from the Overview.
- **全体 (Overview)** — the map button at the top of the left rail:
  - **地図 (Map):** every brain with what its AI is doing, grouped softly by
    個人 / チーム / 組織 when brains have a category. A dashed line joins two
    brains when an AI in one used notes from the other, named by the note.
    Click a brain to open it.
  - **Brain の AI:** beside the map, each brain's AI with its state, the request
    it waits on, **停止** and **送信を再開**. Choose a brain and type to send it
    an instruction, or add it to that brain's queue while it runs.
  - **並列 (Columns):** the brains side by side with their AI, Schema, Knowledge
    and Contents. A note opens in its brain.
- **Search every brain.** ⌘K / Ctrl+K has a **すべての Brain** choice (the default
  when opened from the Overview) that shows results grouped by brain. Searches
  in different brains no longer cancel each other.
- The status bar's AI summary leads to the Overview while another brain's AI is
  working. The workspace can be left once no brain's AI is running.

## Installing

**Windows 11 x64:** download `irori-0.1.41-windows-x64-Setup.exe` and run it. The
installer carries no distribution signature, so Windows shows a warning that the
publisher cannot be verified.

**Mac (Apple silicon):** download `irori-0.1.41-macos-arm64.dmg`, open it, and
drag **irori** into **アプリケーション**. Intel Macs are not supported. The first
launch is refused because the package carries no distribution signature. Open
**システム設定 → プライバシーとセキュリティ**, find irori's blocked notice under
セキュリティ, and press **このまま開く**. Later launches behave normally. Keep
irori in **アプリケーション**: the in-app update replaces the app where it is.

An installed 0.1.29 or later offers this version with **更新して再起動**.

## Verification

Every step ran the full build, unit tests and Electron UI suites; the suites
were rewritten for the new layout without weakening what they prove. New UI
tests cover the brain settings sheet (each part, the two-character limit,
cancel, an image, restart) and the Overview: two protocol-fixture CLIs in two
brains at once, switching brains during a run, a waiting request answered after
switching and from the Overview, a queue going on by itself while another brain
is shown, stop and resume, sending and queueing from the Overview, the map, the
columns, and search across every brain. Unit tests cover the brain declaration
(unknown fields kept, old files, refused looks, links out of the KB), the map
layout and its reference lines, and scans of different KBs running side by
side. No real model runs in these tests.

## Remaining boundaries

Passing CI is not acceptance on an installed device, and real CLIs have not
been run in two brains at once for this release. The Overview sends with each
brain's default access mode. The map's layout is a simple first one. **Your
AI** — the person's own agent at the centre of the map, handing work to each
brain's AI — comes in a later release. A brain's look changed on another device
arrives through Git and shows after the KB is opened again.

# irori 0.1.60 — conversations you keep

Each hibachi and the irori agent now keep any number of conversations, whole,
on this PC. You can start a new one, go back to an earlier one, and name, pin,
archive or delete them.

## Conversations

- **新しい会話** starts an empty conversation on the same CLI, in a hibachi's
  panel and now in the irori agent's too. Choosing another CLI also starts a new
  conversation.
- **履歴** lists the conversations of that hibachi, or of the irori agent, never
  the other's: the title, the CLI, the time and the note it began on, pinned ones
  first and archived ones apart. Group them by note, or show only the open
  note's. Rename, pin, archive or delete one from its row; deleting removes
  irori's copy and leaves the CLI's own history where it is.
- **A conversation's title** is the first line of its first instruction, until
  you rename it.
- **Whole conversations** are kept, not the last 400 events: every instruction
  and reply, each tool's call and result (a tool's output over 1 MiB is kept to
  its first 1 MiB and says so), and how each turn ended.
- **One run per hibachi at a time**: an instruction sent in another conversation
  of a hibachi that is working waits in the queue and starts when the run ends.
  Different hibachis still work at once.
- **Sessions follow the device**: a conversation continues its CLI's session on
  the device, folder and access setting it was made with, and otherwise starts a
  new session and says so. If a session cannot be continued, the next
  instruction starts a new one in the same conversation.
- **A routine's agent step** and each piece of work the irori agent hands to a
  hibachi are conversations of their own, so they no longer mix into yours.
- **会話をリセット** is gone; 新しい会話 takes its place.

Your earlier conversation with each hibachi and CLI appears once as
**以前の会話**, with its history and its waiting instructions. The old files
stay in irori's data folder for this release. Nothing is sent to a model when
they are moved.

## Installing

**Windows 11 x64:** download `irori-0.1.60-windows-x64-Setup.exe` and run it. The
installer carries no distribution signature, so Windows shows a warning that the
publisher cannot be verified.

**Mac (Apple silicon):** download `irori-0.1.60-macos-arm64.dmg`, open it, and
drag **irori** into **アプリケーション**. Intel Macs are not supported. The first
launch is refused because the package carries no distribution signature. Open
**システム設定 → プライバシーとセキュリティ**, find irori's blocked notice under
セキュリティ, and press **このまま開く**. Later launches behave normally. Keep
irori in **アプリケーション**: the in-app update replaces the app where it is.

An installed 0.1.29 or later offers this version with **更新して再起動**.

## Verification

- The full build, unit tests and Electron UI journeys passed, including a new
  one: an earlier conversation moved in, 新しい会話, a send waiting behind another
  conversation's run, rename, pin, archive, delete, a restart, and the irori
  agent's separate history.
- Unit tests cover damaged metadata and event lines, a second device, a moved
  folder, a failed resume and the move of earlier records.
- Real CLIs were not run for this change.

## Remaining boundaries

- Choosing where conversations are kept, and searching them, come next; then
  going back to an earlier message, cloning, and giving a new session the
  conversation so far
  ([ADR 017](https://github.com/DeL-TaiseiOzaki/irori/blob/main/docs/decisions/017-conversation-history.md)
  stages 2 and 3).
- A conversation continued on another device or in another folder starts a new
  session without the earlier turns until stage 3.
- Not yet tried in an installed macOS or Windows 11 app.

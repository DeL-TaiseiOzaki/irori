# irori 0.1.69 — groups of hibachis in the rail

- Hibachis in the rail can be gathered into named groups. Right-click a
  hibachi to start a group or move it into one; click the group to close or
  open it, and right-click the group to rename or ungroup it.
- A closed group is one folder of small tiles. It still shows when one of its
  hibachi agents is working or waiting for approval, and when it holds the
  hibachi on show.
- Groups, and whether each is open, are kept for each workspace on this device.

## Verification

Unit tests cover where a group stands in the rail, membership rules and the
saved groups. A new Electron UI suite groups hibachis, closes a group,
restarts the app with it closed, renames it and ungroups it. Installed
Windows and macOS builds were not run.

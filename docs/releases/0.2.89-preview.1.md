# irori 0.2.89 preview 1 — Sub-sessions sit under their session

- On the session board, a hand-off and a CLI's own sub-agent now stand indented
  under the card of the conversation they belong to, on a rule in the
  sub-session colour. In 0.2.88 the board's list style took that indent and
  rule away, so they lined up with every other card
  ([ADR 069](../decisions/069-session-board.md)).

## Verification

`npm run build` passes. `scripts/session-board-ui-smoke.ts` now checks that a
sub-agent's card stands indented under its parent on the rule, and passes on
Linux under Xvfb; it failed that check before the fix. Not tried on macOS or
Windows.

# irori 0.1.55 — less to read

irori explained itself in many places: a paragraph under a heading, a hint under
a field, two sentences where two words would do. Most of that text is gone. A
screen should be usable without reading it.

## What changed

- **Removed:** descriptions under section headings (Schema instructions, rules,
  hooks and skills; Git; materials and outputs; pending uploads; the irori
  agent), field help that repeated the field, tooltips that repeated a label,
  and "where this is kept" notes.
- **Shortened:** success notices ("Committed.", "Pushed."), empty states
  ("No runs yet."), search and backlink notices ("Some notes could not be
  checked."), the agent access line under the composer, and multi-sentence
  errors.
- **Kept, in one sentence:** what prevents a wrong or irreversible action. This
  covers what a push sends, that a public repository is readable by anyone,
  where a deleted note can be restored, that pending uploads stay on the device,
  and that OpenCode's standard mode runs many tools without approval.

Behaviour, buttons, headings and labels are unchanged.

## Installing

**Windows 11 x64:** download `irori-0.1.55-windows-x64-Setup.exe` and run it. The
installer carries no distribution signature, so Windows shows a warning that the
publisher cannot be verified.

**Mac (Apple silicon):** download `irori-0.1.55-macos-arm64.dmg`, open it, and
drag **irori** into **アプリケーション**. Intel Macs are not supported. The first
launch is refused because the package carries no distribution signature. Open
**システム設定 → プライバシーとセキュリティ**, find irori's blocked notice under
セキュリティ, and press **このまま開く**. Later launches behave normally. Keep
irori in **アプリケーション**: the in-app update replaces the app where it is.

An installed 0.1.29 or later offers this version with **更新して再起動**.

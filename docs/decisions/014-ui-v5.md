# 014 — UI v5: brains, your AI and each brain's AI

Date: 2026-09-25. Status: owner decision; design approved on the canvas,
implemented in stages from 0.1.38. Replaces the five-pane layered explorer of
[LAYERED-EXPLORER](../LAYERED-EXPLORER.md) and the layout sections of
[UI-DESIGN](../UI-DESIGN.md). Keeps [ADR 003](003-ui-foundation.md)'s semantic
tokens (with new values), [ADR 007](007-obsidian-ui.md)'s controls where they
still fit, and [ADR 011](011-interface-language.md)'s `t(ja, en)`.

## Context

The owner asked for a zero-based redesign of the whole interface with no change
to irori's concept or functions: irori is an IDE for managing several "second
brains" — each a set of Schema, Knowledge and Contents — and editing files in
them. The interface should be solid, modern, dynamic, understandable from icons
without explanatory text, and clear to a first-time user.

The five-pane explorer grouped files by layer row and by personal/team column,
so one brain was cut across up to five panes. A design canvas went through five
rounds on 2026-09-25; the owner approved v5 and asked to switch to it entirely
("現在の v5 に全面的に切り替えましょう"). The canvas is the owner's private
Design artifact with English and 日本語 pages of 13 artboards each; its tokens,
markup and motion are the visual reference for the implementation.

## Decision

1. **The brain is the unit.** A registered KB (scope) is shown as a _brain_: its
   Schema, Knowledge and Contents together, in one panel, in that order. The UI
   term is "Brain" in Japanese and "brain" in English; the layer names stay
   Schema / Knowledge / Contents in both languages.
2. **A workspace is any combination of any brains**, in the order its owner
   chose. The personal / team / organization category is an optional property of
   a brain, not a frame of the layout; the Overview map shows it as a soft
   grouping only when brains carry it. How the map arranges brains will be
   designed separately; until then a simple deterministic layout stands in.
3. **Three levels**: Overview (all brains of the workspace) → Brain (one brain's
   three layers, its home, changes, graph and records) → Note. The rail on the
   left holds the Overview entry and the workspace's brains; entering and leaving
   a level is a spatial zoom.
4. **A brain's AI is the CLI agent that has loaded that brain's Schema** —
   irori's existing per-scope session of Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Pi, run
   in the brain's folder. Brains' AIs may run at the same time; the rail and the
   Overview show each one's state.
5. **Your AI** is the person's own agent with its own Schema (a device-level
   folder; the canvas uses `~/irori/you`). You talk to it in the Overview; it
   organises work, hands tasks to brain AIs and receives their reports. Its
   Schema carries the rule that defines each brain's AI as a sub-agent (the
   `brain-agents` skill): load that brain's `AGENTS.md` and `.agents/skills`,
   work only inside that brain's folder, report the result and changed files.
   Claude Code receives the definitions as `.claude/agents/<brain>.md`; Codex has
   native sub-agents (`multi_agent`) without a definition file, so it spawns them
   following the skill's steps. irori does not write these definitions itself;
   it asks your AI to update them.
6. **Brain identity**: each brain has an icon (a symbol from a 24-glyph set, one
   or two characters, or an image) and one of eight colours named 石 藍 苔 青磁
   藤 桜 砂 白 (Stone, Indigo, Moss, Celadon, Wisteria, Sakura, Sand, White).
   Orange is not offered: it belongs to the AI. Name and category become
   editable in the same sheet.
7. **Visual system**: graphite chrome, a paper stage for notes and brain views,
   and one flame accent (ember) reserved for the AI — sparkles, running and
   waiting states, send/allow, and hand-off lines. Non-AI primary buttons are
   solid paper-on-graphite or ink-on-paper. Layers are told apart by icon colour
   (Schema violet, Knowledge in the text colour, Contents teal), never by filled
   areas. Typography: Geist and Geist Mono bundled locally (the renderer's CSP
   allows only local fonts), Japanese through Noto Sans JP or the system
   Japanese font.
8. **Motion**: sparks travel along hand-offs and references, a brain whose AI
   works glows and its rail tile carries a turning ring, a request waiting for
   the person breathes, hover and press respond; all loops stop under reduced
   motion.
9. **New behaviour that comes with v5**: brain AIs run in parallel (the renderer
   no longer locks switching while one runs); search across all brains with
   results grouped by brain (⌘K); answering an AI's request and sending to a
   brain's AI from the Overview; editing a brain's name, category, icon and
   colour.
10. **Both languages are first-class**; every new string goes through `t(ja, en)`.

## Delivery

A short series of stacked pull requests, each shippable and verified on its own:
foundation (tokens, fonts, rail, brain panel, stage chrome, AI panel), brain
views (home, graph, materials, ⌘K, Drive sheet, start), brain identity,
Overview with parallel brain AIs, your AI (after a spike), and motion polish.
Every capability of the 0.1.37 interface is kept; the implementation checks the
mapping item by item against the inventory taken at `31bd1bf`.

## Consequences

- The renderer's layout is rewritten; every Electron UI smoke that selects by
  the old structure is rewritten to the new one without weakening what it
  proves.
- HostAPI grows: update a space's declaration (name, category, appearance);
  search several spaces; your AI's folder and session; observing sub-agent
  activity so tasks show per brain.
- `.irori/scope.json` gains an optional appearance field, since identity is
  shared through the KB; older declarations stay valid.
- Fonts add their OFL notices to THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES and a little to the
  installer.

## Answered by the owner

Asked on 2026-09-25 and answered on 2026-09-26, each with the default the
implementation already used:

| Question                                                    | Answer                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| The default interface language                              | Japanese, as in ADR 011                                 |
| Your AI's folder: location, per person or per workspace     | `~/irori/you`, one per person                           |
| Your AI's starter Schema: from irori-templete or from irori | A minimal starter written by irori                      |
| Whether brain identity is shared through the KB             | Shared: an optional `appearance` in `.irori/scope.json` |

On the your-AI spike ([research](../research/YOUR-AI-SPIKE-2026-09-25.md)) the
owner confirmed decision 5 as intended: your AI, the orchestrating agent, has a
sub-agent per brain whose definition makes it load that brain's Schema, and what
your AI has those sub-agents do is to be designed further. Claude Code comes
first; Codex definitions (`.codex/agents/*.toml`) depend on which subscription a
person's main agent uses and follow later. Real-provider acceptance runs for
this stage are authorized.

## Brain identity (0.1.40)

Implemented with the default for the third question: `.irori/scope.json` carries
an optional `appearance` with `icon` (`{kind: 'glyph', glyph}`, `{kind: 'text',
text}` of one or two characters, or `{kind: 'image', path}` naming
`.irori/icon-<sha256 prefix>.<ext>`) and `color` (one of the eight). `category` is
optional so that a brain can carry none; irori 0.1.39 and earlier cannot open a
declaration without one. irori rewrites the file only from the brain settings,
keeping fields it does not know.

## Overview (0.1.41)

The Overview ships before your AI. Its map lays brains out in rows by category
(organization, team, personal, then none) in the workspace's order, with soft
areas for categories and dashed reference lines where a run in one brain used
another workspace brain's notes as sources; `src/domain/overview.ts` is the only
place that decides positions, so the owner's later map design replaces it
there. Until your AI arrives, the island beside the map is **Brain の AI**: each
brain's AI with its state, the request it waits on, stop and resume, and a
composer that sends to a chosen brain's AI (or queues behind its run). The
columns view shows AI / Schema / Knowledge / Contents per brain; the brain
waiting for the person carries an ember ring.
